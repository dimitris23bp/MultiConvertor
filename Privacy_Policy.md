# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **CryptoConverter** ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

We respect your privacy. Our business model is not based on collecting or selling your personal data.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Data
**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII)** such as your name, email address, phone number, or location data.

### Usage Data
We do not use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Mixpanel). We rely solely on the standard, anonymized **App Analytics** provided by Apple through App Store Connect to understand basic app usage (e.g., crash reports, download numbers). This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.

## 2. Data Storage and Synchronization

### Local Storage
All user preferences, such as your list of favorite cryptocurrencies, are stored locally on your device using **SwiftData**. This data remains completely under your control and is not shared with us or any third-party servers.

### CloudKit (Public Data)
The App fetches cryptocurrency market data (such as prices and logos) from a public database hosted on **Apple CloudKit**.
*   **Reading Data:** When the App updates prices, it requests data from Apple's servers.
*   **User Identity:** You do not need to sign in to iCloud to use the App, and the App does not associate your usage with your iCloud account.
*   **Server Interaction:** The App acts purely as a "reader" of this public data. It does not upload any of your personal content to CloudKit.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following services to function:

*   **Apple Services:** The App is distributed via the Apple App Store and uses Apple CloudKit for data delivery. Apple’s handling of data is governed by their own [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 4. Children’s Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can resolve the issue.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
